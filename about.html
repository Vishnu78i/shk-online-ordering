The Story Behind SHK

SHUDH KHANNA WITH SHK WALA
MORE THAN A MEAL

Food that feels like home.

Swastik Home Kitchen is built around a simple belief: some of the most memorable food does not need to be complicated. It needs to feel honest, familiar and made with care.

SHK brings the warmth of a home kitchen to Namma Bengaluru, celebrating the comfort of Indian food through flavours that invite you to slow down, share a table and enjoy one more bite.

From warm breads and hearty gravies to fragrant rice and refreshing accompaniments, our story is about food with personality — food that carries a sense of place, tradition and togetherness.

“Shudh khanna is not just about what is on the plate. It is about how the plate makes you feel.”
Comfort on the tableFrom everyday breads to rich gravies and fragrant rice, every SHK plate is designed to feel warm, generous and familiar.
HOMELY • WARM • AUTHENTIC

Our philosophy

We want every SHK experience to feel welcoming from the first glance. That means approachable food, a warm visual identity, clear ordering and a brand personality that feels rooted in Indian homes rather than distant from them.

Our promise

We keep coming back to the basics: familiar flavours, comforting combinations, thoughtful presentation and the joy of sharing. SHK is designed to grow with its community while keeping that homestyle feeling at the centre.
